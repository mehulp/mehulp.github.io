Mouse
Click a bubble: a page opens, a section or tag shows its pages. Hover for a preview. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
Touch
Tap a bubble for a preview, then Open page. Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom.
Keyboard
Tab to the graph, then use the arrow keys to move between bubbles, Enter or Space to choose one and Esc to close a card. Rotating and zooming need a mouse or touch.
Anywhere
Click the name or the centre bubble to reset the view. List shows every page as text, and Search jumps to a page or tag.
