These projects started with things I was trying to solve for myself while preparing for interviews.

I needed a better way to organise my learning resources, practise answering interview questions, and evaluate job opportunities. Instead of relying entirely on existing tools, I started building my own.

What began as small personal experiments gradually turned into applications that I could actually use. They also gave me a chance to get back into coding, explore different technologies, and learn by building.

These are ongoing projects. Some are more complete than others, and I'm continuing to improve them as I learn.
